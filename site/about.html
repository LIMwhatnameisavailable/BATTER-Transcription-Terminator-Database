BTED

About the database

Purpose

BTED makes public bacterial transcript 3′-end datasets easier to find, compare, download, and inspect in a genome browser.

Current scope

The v0.2.0 demonstration covers 22 sources listed in BATTER Table S1: 21 endpoint datasets and one metadata-only audit record.
